Here are several user stories that illustrate how users would interact with this agentic finance manager app to track spending and achieve their financial goals:

**1. Automated Categorization and Budget Tracking**

* **As a user,** I want to securely upload my bank transaction CSVs so that the app can automatically clean the data and accurately map cryptic vendor codes to standardized expense categories (e.g., dining, utilities, entertainment).
* **As a user,** I want the app to proactively alert me when I am approaching my self-defined spending limits (e.g., "You have spent 70% of your monthly grocery budget") and suggest immediate areas where I can reduce discretionary spending.

**2. Predictive Forecasting and Scenario Planning**

* **As a user,** I want to ask the agent hypothetical questions—such as "How will a 10% increase in my rent affect my ability to buy a car in three years?"—so the app can simulate the financial impact and adjust my long-term savings plan.
* **As a user,** I want the app to analyze my historical cash flow and provide a forecasted baseline of my recurring income and bills, allowing me to strategically plan for future expenses.

**3. Goal Optimization and Wealth Management**

* **As a user,** I want the agent to evaluate my categorized transaction data against my stated financial goals to recommend an optimized monthly budget allocation that maximizes my savings rate.
* **As a user,** I want the app to synthesize my spending behavior and risk profile to offer hyper-personalized wealth management and investment recommendations, delivered in accessible, jargon-free language.

**4. Anomaly Detection and Dispute Resolution**

* **As a user,** I want the app to continuously monitor my transaction history using pattern recognition to immediately flag unusual spending, duplicate charges, or potential fraud.
* **As a user,** I want the agent to help me quickly resolve an incorrect charge by autonomously pulling the relevant transaction data and drafting a personalized dispute message to my bank.
